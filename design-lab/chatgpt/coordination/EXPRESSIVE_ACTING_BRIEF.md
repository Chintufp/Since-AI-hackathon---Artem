## Animation acceptance criteria

Each act has a readable intention, anticipation, action, reaction and return. Props arrive and depart deliberately. Hands touch the thing they hold. A planted foot stays planted through the load-bearing portion of a motion. Turning changes the face, body silhouette and limb overlap; a profile has one visible eye. Landing has compression, recovery and a small settle. Emotions follow the act rather than random face swapping.

For the moonwalk: glance, produce hat, place it, front-to-three-quarter-to-profile turn, alternating heel/toe weight transfer, backward glide, brim tip, front-facing finish and orderly prop recovery.

Every pass records concrete deficiencies and the subsequent changes. Preserve intermediate sources and comparable frame strips. Technical checks include the recently fixed early-animation-timestamp regression; do not regress it. No final perfection claim is expected: show the improved batch for user taste and pacing feedback.

The preferred click greeting remains intact. Reduced motion, interruption, offscreen cancellation, no autoplay and quiet optional scheduling remain required. No production app code is in scope.
