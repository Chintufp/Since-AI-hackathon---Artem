The learning contract we still need to settle

Small supervised models and a decision policy have different targets. These definitions keep the RL proposal concrete.

State and available actions

State: candidate explanations, evidence support, missing checks, asset/environment, available tools, eligible recipients, prior answers and remaining question budget.

Actions: choose a permitted check, choose a question and eligible recipient, wait, show a supported recommendation, or abstain. The policy cannot invent operational authority.

Observations and transitions

A new measurement or attributed answer updates the evidence state. A Yes/No answer concerns its exact question. Unknown and skip leave a gap; they are not negative truth labels.

A bandit treats each choice as a context-dependent decision. Sequential RL models how one choice changes the information available for later decisions.

Reward: a target, not a vote count

For a recommendation asked about as “Did this help?”, Yes=1 and No=0 is a usable preference target. For “Did traffic fail again?”, No is an observation, not automatically reward zero.

Question utility needs a measured decision benefit, not just agreement or lower model uncertainty. Optimize useful assistance per eligible opportunity with error, unnecessary-interruption and burden limits. Track missed useful opportunities so silence cannot win by default. Weights and thresholds remain open.

Training and release sequence

Generate documented scenario families → obtain LLM/audited labels → train supervised baselines → log eligible actions and outcomes → evaluate a bandit → consider sequential RL only if multi-step benefit is demonstrated.

Train on versioned batches. Evaluate on separate cases. Compare in shadow mode. Promote or roll back. A new human answer updates memory before any model retraining.

From MCP exposure to useful outcomes

Log context returned, agent-reported use, human helpfulness and observed outcomes as different events. Join by decision/trace ID and claim version. An MCP read or the agent's own rating is not independent proof of usefulness.

Report useful coverage, unnecessary proactive offers, missed useful opportunities and expert burden. Unanswered feedback remains unknown; audit abstentions as well as shown suggestions.

Choose a question, person and time

One coordinator ranks eligible actions using decision impact, firsthand answerability, source independence and burden. Defer/no-question is an action. Reserve assignments and cancel obsolete questions.

Binary answers update individual claims or relationships. Different engineers may describe different time windows; do not turn a majority vote into truth or reward points into credibility.
