## Motion language

- White pixel-circle identity and small readable face remain recognizable. Pixel props/temporary limbs are welcome. Authored silhouettes, crisp integer-grid positions, nearest-neighbor display; no blurred/vector tween artwork.
- Familiar gestures can carry pop-culture references. Keep the companion itself consistent and readable at its actual small size. Do not replace its identity with a different mascot for every joke.
- Distinct anticipation, act, reaction, and settle. Give failed tricks a warm recovery; never use shame or injury as an obligation to interact.
- Preview finite clips individually, with frame stepping or pause. A separate explicit Play idle loop can audition combinations. No autoplay on load or on design selection.
- Actual quiet mode: small nearby wandering, long varied rests, infrequent performances. No crossing typing or click targets, no focus stealing, and no apparent desktop-content surveillance. Curiosity can react to pointer/input provided by the app, without implying the avatar read unrelated windows.
- Stop a performance immediately for input, question interaction, window drag, reduced motion, hidden/offscreen previews, or user Stop. Preserve meaningful still poses for reduced motion.
- No, Later and Dismiss receive equal warmth. Tricks are independent of points or answer frequency.
- Entrance comparison: deeper pancake squash, short recovery overshoot and readable dizzy/stars beat. Preserve the already-liked click animation.
