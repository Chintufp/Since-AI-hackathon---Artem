## Not verified / limits

- The points view was captured in the mock only; it was not opened in the real desktop app against the real service in this session (the HTTP path it uses was exercised by hand and by tests).
- A person the judge switches to mid-day may already have been opted in by the stream (it spares only the person chosen when the day started or when that person first appeared); they can opt out in the view. The seeded demo people (Erkki, Veikko, …) are opted in by the stream once the day starts, so the DEMO.md paragraph should be done as a person the judge *chooses* to opt in (Erkki before the stream starts, or any person: the toggle is theirs).
- The simulated directory lists a few non-person entries ("Field Team"); the stream opts them in like anyone else. Harmless for the rating, but a real directory filter would exclude groups.
- "Correction" points are given for observations recorded on one's own investigation (`POST /observations`); there is no separate correction route in the engine, so claim corrections (`memory/corrections.ts`) earn nothing yet.
- Evidence awards consider only the claims present at import time; a claim extracted later from the same evidence (none in this pipeline) would not award retroactively.
- Screenshot sequence numbers after the Settings state shift by two (the two new captures); the committed baselines in `docs/runs/2026-11-07-overnight/screenshots/` were not regenerated here (the runs wrote to a scratch folder); only the points captures were copied in, under `screenshots/points/`.
