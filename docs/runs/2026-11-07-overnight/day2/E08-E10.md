Every question of the day touched the new train/validation cases: 5 cases become 17. Questions per point gained: undefined (n/a), because the same-case product did not move.
- Questions asked: 9 per selector (3 per seed, the same questions as before the change). Answered: rules 5/9, LinUCB 6/9. Mean reward (delta minus burden): rules -0.008 (was -0.0215), LinUCB -0.0033 (was -0.0259); what remains is the burden term alone.
- The two scores apart (new, per answer, on the touched case): the applicability answers raised completeness on 3/5 answers for rules (sum +12.49 points: one key of four of the distinguishing slot is 1/24 = 4.17 points) and 4/6 for LinUCB (sum +16.66 points); correctness moved 0 points on every answer.
