- False merges: rules 0/7 (train) and 1/4 (validation); union the same. Model alone through commit: 1/8 and 1/5. False splits for the model alone: 4/7 (direct) and 2/7 (commit) on train; the union has 0/7.
- **What the model found that the rules missed**: 3 truth claims ("Do not roll back N5 units, apply the v7.1 patch..." advice; "Seppo re-dressed the cabinet cabling..." action; "Swap the PSU and keep an eye on the weak mains..." advice). 2 of them still count through the union. The union commits 23 (train) + 17 (validation) model-only claims. Most of them match no truth claim, which is why union precision falls.
- **What the validator dropped**: 11 items. 4 claims cited a message id that does not exist, 1 claim and 1 entity had an excerpt that is not in the message, 3 links cited an unknown message and 2 links had a non-verbatim excerpt. No thread produced unusable output (0/24).
- **Time**: 24 calls, mean 36.5 s, max 86.4 s. 4/24 calls alone go over the 60 s slot budget, and **6/6 imports go over it** when the calls are summed (124-193 s per family). With the live budget, every one of these imports falls back to rules. The experiment lifts the budget so that the model's output can be scored.
- For the slide: the model is a modest add-on, not a replacement. Through the union it adds one truth claim per split (+2 points of recall on train, +5 on validation) and costs precision. It cannot fit the 60 s budget on this Mac.

## E10: the store metric over a simulated day, LinUCB vs rules

Each arm uses a fresh demo store (synthetic), runs the demo:play day for 10 h in-process against the real service, and uses 3 seeds. Both selectors get the same day.

- Demo store before: 5 cases, completeness 0.45, correctness 0.0167. Only 1 of 60 case claims rests on ≥2 independent sources or an observed outcome; summed over 3 seeds that is 3/180. Product: 0.0075.
- After the day, on the same 5 cases, nothing has changed for either selector on any seed (gain 0 points). Every question of the day touched the new train/validation cases: 5 cases become 17. Questions per point gained: undefined, because nothing was gained.
- Questions asked: 9 per selector (3 per seed). Answered: rules 5/9, LinUCB 6/9. Measured per-answer deltas: rules 0/5 positive (sum -6.77 points), LinUCB 0/6 positive (sum -13.54 points). Mean reward: rules -0.0215, LinUCB -0.0259.
- Bandit vs rules (frozen: a higher same-case gain per question on ≥2 of 3 seeds): LinUCB wins **0/3**. Rules stay the default.
- Why the deltas are negative (D-B2-51): each non-zero delta is a "no" to "does the earlier unit share this unit's model?" The coordinator stores that answer as a contradict update on the historical claim. The store metric counts it as a contradicted claim that a recommendation still cites, so correctness goes below 0. That literally matches brief 5.2, but the coordinator's own note says the historical claim is unchanged. It is an open reading, not a fix made here.
- For the slide: the metric is measured and is wired into the bandit's reward. On one simulated day it does not move. That has two causes: the demo store's claims are almost all single-source, and the day's questions go to the new cases. This is an honest "not yet", not a win.
