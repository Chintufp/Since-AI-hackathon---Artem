Lumi · pilot and continuation plan

Artem · Since AI Hackathon 2026 · Elisa challenge

Lumi catches fixes that live only in chat, checks them with the people who know through one Yes/No question at a time, and serves the confirmed knowledge to colleagues and to AI agents over MCP. The prototype runs end to end on one machine. The next step is to prove it with real engineers: an eight-week pilot with one Elisa operations team.

Where we are today

Working: capture from Slack, Teams exports, e-mail and paste; claims bound to equipment, people and time; retrieval and investigation; one-question-per-person under budgets and quiet hours; the Lumi companion (macOS, Windows build); an MCP server with 12 tools; 641 automated tests. Not yet shown: real engineers answering, a live channel over weeks, and deciding reliably whether old advice applies to a new unit (our weakest step on synthetic data).

What we measure

  • Answer rate and seconds of expert time per answer.
  • Knowledge kept: cases with a confirmed fix and source, per week.
  • Reuse: searches and agent calls that found an earlier confirmed fix.
  • Errors: wrongly merged cases, advice marked as confirmed when it wasn’t (target: none in the reviewed sample).
  • Engineer opinion: would they keep Lumi on?

What we build during the pilot

  • Live connectors hardened for the chosen channel and mailbox.
  • Better linking of new messages to open cases, trained on the pilot’s reviewed same/different pairs.
  • Applicability checks: does this fix fit this unit, firmware, site?
  • Question selection learned from real answers, kept only if it beats the rules on held-out cases.

What we need from Elisa

  • A sponsor and one team lead; 8–15 volunteer engineers.
  • Read-only access to one channel and one mailbox label.
  • A place to run the service, and approval of the model boundary.

Guardrails

  • Nothing leaves Elisa’s environment; private channels stay private.
  • Lumi only reads; it never posts or sends on anyone’s behalf.
  • Questions are capped per person and paused off-hours; one click turns Lumi off.

After the pilot

If the numbers hold: extend to more teams and channels (Teams, e-mail, GitHub, ticketing), add single sign-on and per-team permissions, and offer the MCP interface to Elisa’s own agents as their source of confirmed operational knowledge.
